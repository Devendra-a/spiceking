The Spice King Experience

A Welcoming Highway Dining Destination in Chandauli

Located on the Grand Trunk Road (NH-19) connecting Varanasi, Saidraja, and Mohania, Spice King offers a calm, hygienic dining experience for travelers and families alike.

Prepared with traditional spices, slow fire cooking, and fresh dairy ingredients, our menu focuses on authentic North Indian flavors. Guests can choose between our climate-controlled indoor dining hall and an expansive open garden lawn.
