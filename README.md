## How to Run & Show the Demo to the Owner

1. **Direct Offline Demo (No Internet or Server Required):**
   - Double-click on `index.html` in this folder to open it directly in Chrome, Edge, or Brave.
   - You can also open `menu.html` to show the standalone QR Table Menu with live search.

2. **Test on Mobile (Simulating the Customer Experience):**
   - Press `F12` in Chrome/Edge, click the device toggle icon (or `Ctrl + Shift + M`), and select "iPhone 14 Pro" or "Samsung Galaxy".
   - Notice the **sticky floating bottom bar** ("Call", "WhatsApp", "Route", "Reserve") designed specifically for highway travelers and diners!

3. **Deploy Free on the Web in 2 Minutes:**
   - **Option A (Netlify Drop):** Go to [netlify.com/drop](https://app.netlify.com/drop) and drag-and-drop the entire `spice-king-restaurant` folder. You will get a live link like `spiceking.netlify.app` within 30 seconds!
   - **Option B (Vercel):** Run `vercel` from this folder or import to GitHub.
   - **Option C (GitHub Pages):** Push to a GitHub repository and turn on GitHub Pages.

## Owner Pitching Strategy (Owner ko kaise impress karein)

Jab aap Spice King ke owner se milein, to unhe ye points highlight kijiye:

1. **Real Photos of Their Restaurant (Unhi ke restaurant ki asli photos):**
   - Show unka AC Dining Hall with wooden flooring & brick pillars (`spice_king_2.jpg`).
   - Show unka Lush Green Garden Lawn daylight view (`spice_king_6.jpg`).
   - Show Birthday party celebration setup with Happy Birthday gold decoration (`spice_king_4.jpg` & `spice_king_5.jpg`).
   - Show evening lawn dinner (`spice_king_3.jpg`) and Highway parking area (`spice_king_1.jpg`).
   - *Owner will instantly feel connected because it's genuinely their restaurant, not a fake template.*

2. **Direct WhatsApp Ordering (0% Commission):**
   - Swiggy aur Zomato 25% to 30% commission lete hain.
   - Is website se diner menu dekhta hai, "Add to Plate" karta hai aur ek click par **WhatsApp pe poora order total bill ke sath direct owner ke phone (+91 62069 97192)** par chala jata hai!
   - Isme Dine-in (Table No.), Highway Takeaway (Packing), aur Pre-order options shamil hain.

3. **Digital QR Code Table Menu (`menu.html`):**
   - Har table par ek QR Code lagaya ja sakta hai jise scan karke customer menu dekh sake.
   - Search bar se customer turant "Paneer", "Chicken", "Biryani", "Roti" khoj sakta hai.
   - Paper menu bar-bar print karane ka kharcha khatam.

4. **Birthday Party & Banquet Lawn Lead Generation:**
   - Lawn aur Hall me birthday, anniversary aur family get-together book karne ke liye dedicated booking form hai jo direct WhatsApp lead generate karta hai.

5. **Live Business Status Indicator:**
   - Real-time time check karke website dikhati hai "Open Now | Closes at 12:15 AM" ya "Closed Now | Opens at 8:00 AM".
